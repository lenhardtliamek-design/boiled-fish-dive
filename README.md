# Boiled Fish Dive · 水煮鱼

A first-person endless runner inspired by *Infinite Pizza*: dive down an endless tunnel of Sichuan chili oil, slip through gaps in walls of tofu, fish and vegetables, and blast red chili walls with a peppercorn blaster. It keeps speeding up until you crash.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's a single file; it loads three.js and Google Fonts from CDNs, so it needs an internet connection.

## Controls

- **Computer:** ← → or A / D to steer, Space or click to shoot, Space or R to dive again, Esc or P to pause
- **Phone:** drag to steer, tap or press FIRE to shoot

## How it works

- Red chili walls break when you shoot them; everything else you have to dodge.
- **Golden peppers** add bonus distance (more for several in a row).
- Near misses and blasted walls add bonus distance.
- Four spice levels: 微辣 Mild, 中辣 Medium, 特辣 Extra hot, 变态辣 Insane. Reach 1,500 m on one to unlock the next. Best distances save in your browser.
